## Entry

> "A tall stranger will admire you from the street this week. It is the sun, and it has been watching you grow."

**Title: 1 pot, 1 window, 1 tall stranger**

## Why it works

The fortune is written for this plant, in this window, and no other: a stranger admires it from the street, which only makes sense for a plant sitting in a windowsill where passers-by can see it. The classic fortune-teller opening ("a tall stranger will...") sets up the prediction cadence, then the reveal — the stranger is the sun, which has been watching the plant grow — lands as a warm joke a plant lover would smile at, not a groan. It is kind throughout: the plant is admired, watched over, and growing. No doom, no withering, no mockery, just sunlight with a crush.
